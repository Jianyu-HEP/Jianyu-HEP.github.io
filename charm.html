Λc+ → Λπ⁺π⁺π⁻

Charm-baryon amplitude analysis

Question

What dynamics shape Λc⁺ → Λπ⁺π⁺π⁻ decays?

Method

I lead a multibody Λc+ amplitude analysis, using the structure of the decay to study the dynamics encoded in its multidimensional phase space.

My work spans amplitude-model implementation, multidimensional helicity-amplitude fits, large-scale simulation and experimental validation, including TF-PWA / TensorFlow workflows with GPU acceleration.

I also served as LHCb Charm Working Group MC liaison during 2024–2026, supporting production and validation across charm analyses.

Status

Analysis in progress; no results are reported here.

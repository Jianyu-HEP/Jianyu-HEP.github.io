BESIIISCIENCE · 2026

Λ electric dipole moment

Question

Can entangled decays probe the EDM of a particle too short-lived for conventional spin-precession measurements?

Method

The full angular distribution of J/ψ → ΛΛ̅ decays gives access to EDM-sensitive spin correlations.

Published result|dΛ| < 7.0 × 10−19 e cm

95% confidence level · consistent with zero.
Previous upper limit improved by about three orders of magnitude.

My contribution

I contributed to the full angular extraction, data-driven detector studies and propagation of systematic effects through the complete fit.

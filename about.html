About me

I am a postdoctoral fellow at the National Centre for Nuclear Research in Poland. My research connects baryon spin, fundamental symmetries and heavy-flavour dynamics.

My analysis work combines angular and amplitude fits, detector-efficiency calibration and systematic validation at BESIII and LHCb.

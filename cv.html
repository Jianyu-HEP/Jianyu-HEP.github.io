Research profile

I study baryon spin, fundamental symmetries and heavy-flavour dynamics with BESIII and LHCb. My work combines precision multidimensional angular analysis, CP and electric-dipole-moment measurements, data-driven efficiency calibration and systematic validation. At LHCb, I lead a multibody charm-baryon amplitude analysis.

Selected research contributions

  • Λ electric dipole moment: contributed to the full angular extraction using entangled J/ψ → ΛΛ̅ decays, data-driven detector studies and full-fit systematic propagation. The BESIII result is consistent with zero, reaches the 10⁻¹⁹ e cm scale and improves the previous upper limit by about three orders of magnitude (Science, 2026).
  • Angular method for hyperon EDM searches: contributed to the development and experimental implementation of a full angular and spin-correlation strategy for short-lived hyperons (Physical Review D, 2023).
  • Precision CP tests: played leading roles in hyperon decay-parameter and CP-asymmetry measurements with entangled ΛΛ̅ and Ξ⁰Ξ̅⁰ systems and hyperon-to-neutron decays, including efficiency calibration and systematic validation at the 10⁻³ precision scale.
  • Multidimensional analysis software: developed a RooFit framework with unbinned fits, pseudo-data generation, CPU multithreading and precomputed detector-acceptance integrals; adopted in nine additional BESIII analyses.
  • Hyperon interactions: co-developed collider strategies using detector material to study secondary hyperon scattering and antihyperon–proton annihilation.
  • Charm amplitude analysis: leading the ongoing Λc+ → Λπ⁺π⁺π⁻ analysis at LHCb, with experience in multidimensional amplitude models, large-scale simulation and collaboration validation.

Experimental collaborations & service

  • BESIII, 2017–present: hyperon spin, decay parameters, CP/EDM measurements and hyperon–nucleon interactions. Member of internal review committees for seven collaboration papers; regular data-acquisition shifts and detector/systematic studies.
  • LHCb, 2022–present: multibody charm amplitude analysis. Charm Working Group MC liaison during 2024–2026, supporting simulation production, validation and cross-analysis coordination.

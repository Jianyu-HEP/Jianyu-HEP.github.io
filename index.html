Experimental particle physics

Jianyu Zhang.

Postdoctoral Fellow · NCBJ, Poland

I study hyperon spin, CP symmetry and electric dipole moments with BESIII and charm-baryon decay dynamics with LHCb. My work combines multidimensional angular and amplitude analyses, data-driven efficiency calibration and systematic validation to turn collision data into precision tests of fundamental physics.

BESIII

BESIII studies electron–positron collisions at BEPCII in the tau–charm energy region. Its rich baryon samples provide a laboratory for spin correlations, symmetry tests and hadron interactions.

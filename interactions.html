BESIIIHADRON INTERACTIONS

Hyperon–nucleon interactions

Question

Can secondary interactions reveal how hyperons scatter from nucleons?

Method

Hyperons produced at an electron–positron collider can interact with detector material. Reconstructing these events gives access to scattering and antihyperon annihilation.

Result · ΛN scattering · PRC 2024Λp → Σ⁺n

The pπ⁰ mass distribution identifies the Σ⁺ contribution in a study of ΛN inelastic scattering.

My contribution to this programme

I co-developed strategies using detector material to study secondary hyperon scattering and antihyperon–proton annihilation, connecting reconstruction with material and acceptance.
